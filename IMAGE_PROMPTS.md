# Image Prompts for Vancouver Olive Oil Company

These prompts are designed to create a consistent, warm, editorial Mediterranean/PNW aesthetic. Use these in Midjourney (append `--ar 16:9` or `--ar 4:5` as needed) or DALL-E.

1. **Hero Header (Home)**: 
   - *Prompt*: A high-angle, cinematic close-up of vibrant golden extra virgin olive oil being poured slowly from a narrow glass spout onto a fresh, rustic sourdough bread slice. Warm golden hour sunlight streaming through a window. Soft steam rising from the bread. Background is a warm cream linen tablecloth. Cinematic, high detail, commercial food photography style.
   
2. **The Tasting Room Interior**:
   - *Prompt*: Interior photography of a boutique tasting room. Rows of gleaming large stainless steel fustis (tanks) with brass spigots lined up on a dark wood counter. Warm, soft interior lighting. Exposed brick walls and potted olive trees. Minimalist, artisanal, clean, Pacific Northwest craft aesthetic.

3. **Hands Tasting (Ritual)**:
   - *Prompt*: Close-up of a pair of hands gently cupping a small blue cobalt glass tasting cup. Soft focus background of a wooden counter. The scene feels intimate and generous. Natural soft lighting.

4. **Balsamic Drip (Macro)**:
   - *Prompt*: Extreme macro photograph of a single thick, syrupy drop of dark blackberry balsamic vinegar hanging from an antique silver spoon. The vinegar is dark, glossy, and dense. Background is a blurred warm stone texture.

5. **Olive Grove Still Life**:
   - *Prompt*: A minimalist still life featuring a few gnarled olive branches with silvery leaves and small green fruit, resting on a rough cream paper background. Soft, diffused lighting from the side. Editorial aesthetic.

6. **Kitsilano Shop Exterior**:
   - *Prompt*: Daytime exterior shot of a charming boutique storefront on a Vancouver street. Large glass windows showing warm interior light. A small chalkboard sign outside. Flourishing green plants in pots nearby. Clean architecture.

7. **Individual Bottle Portraits**:
   - *Prompt*: A series of dark glass bottles with minimalist cream labels, standing on a rough stone surface. The bottles are backlit by warm sun, creating a glow in the oil. Very clean, high-end product photography.

### Color Palette Notes for Generation:
- Primary: Olive Green (#3D441E)
- Secondary: Golden Amber (#C5A059)
- Neutral: Warm Cream and Stone
- Accent: Deep Balsamic Burgundy
